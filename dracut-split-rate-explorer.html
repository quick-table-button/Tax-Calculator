For illustrative purposes only. Rates are dollars per $1,000 of assessed value. Values are Dracut’s FY2027 totals by class from the Massachusetts Division of Local Services: residential $6.086B, commercial $292M, industrial $78M, personal property $141M. The model starts from today’s single rate of $9.67 per $1,000 and applies it to those FY2027 values, which implies a levy of about $63.8M. The rate and levy fields are linked: change either one and the other updates. This is a what-if, not a certified FY2027 levy. Typical values are FY2025 parcel averages scaled by class growth. The business table uses fixed assessed values: $100,000, $250,000, $500,000, $750,000, then $1M to $5M in $250,000 steps. The home table spans $450,000 to $1.35M in $50,000 steps. State law caps the shift at 1.75×, meaning business can bear at most 175% of its share of value. Rates are not rounded to the cent and exemptions, debt exclusions and overrides are not modeled. Adopting a split rate requires a public hearing and a Select Board vote.
